Deposit flow

On submit you join the shortest open admin booth queue. Steps: 1) wait in booth line, 2) allow cashout on your phone, then tap "I have allowed cashout", 3) admin MoMo script, 4) pay on your phone when prompted — if you cannot pay or enter your PIN, use PIN entry failed or Unable to pay (not wallet claim), 5) claim wallet credit with your MoMo transaction ID after successful payment, 6) submit a new request if the admin script fails (deposit:request-failed).
